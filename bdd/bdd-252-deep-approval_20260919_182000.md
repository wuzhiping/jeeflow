# BDD #252 多层级审批 + 变量累加 (20260919 182000)

## 流程设计
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    start([开始]) --> apply[user1]
    apply --> leader[组长]
    leader --> manager[经理]
    manager --> director[总监]
    director --> boss[总经理]
    boss --> end([结束])
```

## 测试结果

每步提交时累加 `f_amount` (每次 +100) 并标记 `f_<step>_approved=True`:

| 步骤 | 操作人 | 提交 f_amount | state | 累积变量 |
|------|--------|---------------|-------|----------|
| apply (auto) | user1 | (跳过) | 10 | f_amount=1000 (init) |
| leader | leader | 1200 | 10 | f_leader_approved=True |
| manager | manager | 1300 | 10 | f_manager_approved=True |
| director | director | 1400 | 10 | f_director_approved=True |
| boss | boss | 1500 | **20** | f_boss_approved=True |

## 验证

最终 instance state=20, tasks=5 (apply/leader/manager/director/boss)

变量完整保留：
- f_amount = 1500 (累加成功)
- f_leader/manager/director/boss_approved = True (各步标记)
- f_init_amount = 1000 (起始)
- f_step = "started" (起始)
- u_* 操作人字段 (来自 user_prov)

## 结论
- ✅ 5 节点深链工作正常
- ✅ f_* 变量跨节点传递 + 累加
- ✅ u_* 操作人字段 (user_prov) 自动注入
