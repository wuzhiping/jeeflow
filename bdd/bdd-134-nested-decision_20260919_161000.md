# BDD #134 多决策嵌套 (20260919 161000)

## 流程设计
```mermaid
flowchart LR
    start([开始]) --> apply[申请]
    apply --> type_dec{类型}
    type_dec -->|category=1| amount_dec{金额}
    type_dec -->|category=2| leader_a[主管A]
    type_dec -->|category=3| manager_a[经理A]
    type_dec -->|category>=4| director_a[总监A]
    amount_dec -->|amount>5000| level_dec{级别}
    amount_dec -->|amount<=5000| leader_a
    level_dec -->|level=1| boss_a[老板]
    level_dec -->|level>=2| manager_a
    leader_a --> end([结束])
    manager_a --> end
    director_a --> end
    boss_a --> end
```

## 场景
3 层 decision 嵌套：category → amount → level 动态路由

## 测试用例

| Case | category | amount | level | 期望 | 实测 | 结论 |
|------|----------|--------|-------|------|------|------|
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | leader_a (amount<=5000) | leader_a | ✅ |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 8000 | leader_a (category=2) | leader_a | ✅ |
| 3 | 3 | 1 | 3000 | manager_a (category=3) | manager_a | ✅ |
| 4 | 4 | 4 | 1 | director_a (category>=4) | director_a | ✅ |
